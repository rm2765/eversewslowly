We respect your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use, and share information on this site.

### Information We Collect

We collect two types of information:

* **Information You Provide:** We collect information you voluntarily provide, such as when you fill out a form, leave a comment, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, and any other details you submit.
* **Automatically Collected Information:** When you visit the site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity. This includes your IP address, browser type, time zone, and information about the pages you view. This data, often called "Device Information," is collected through cookies and other tracking technologies. We use Google Analytics to track site usage and demographic metrics in order to better serve site visitors.

### How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

* **Operate and Improve the Site:** We use information you provide to respond to your inquiries and to process comments. Automatically collected data is used to improve and optimize the site, screen for potential risk and fraud, and generate analytics about how our customers browse and interact with the site.
* **Communicate with You:** We may use your contact information to send you updates or respond to your questions.
* **Legal Compliance:** We may also share your Personal Information to comply with applicable laws and regulations, to respond to a subpoena, search warrant or other lawful request for information we receive, or to otherwise protect our rights.

We do not currently sell your personal information nor have plans to share your information with third parties.

### Cookies

We use cookies to make our site work correctly and to personalize your experience.

* **Essential Cookies:** These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be switched off in our systems.
* **Optional Cookies:** We use optional cookies for analytics and personalization.

### Third-Party Services & Links

Some services we use, such as analytics tools and content delivery networks, may collect limited data under their own privacy policies. This site may also contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy statements.

* **Affiliate Links:** This site may use affiliate links. If you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a commission. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies regarding the information they collect.

* **Anti-Spam Tools:** We use anti-spam tools, including Netlify Forms extra spam protection, to protect our forms from automated spam submissions. Information submitted through our forms may be screened by these tools for this purpose.

### Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information.

* **GDPR:** If you are a resident of the European Union, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data.
* **CCPA:** If you are a California resident, you may have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, or to request a change or removal of your information, please email us at contact@eversewslowly.com.

### Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
